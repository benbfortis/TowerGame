# Holy Trinity (Scenario 10)
**Focus:** multi-front pressure (3-faction)
**Difficulty:** medium
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize the central mountain pushes both AI factions to fight over n2 and n3, leaving the path to one base open.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: capture n1 (central medium) with P -> n1.
2. T+~3s: n1 captures. Drag P -> n2 (left light) to anchor the left flank.
3. n2 captures. AI and Yellow can't cleanly attack each other across the centre mountain — they fight for n3 and n2.
4. Stream from n1 (medium) -> A. Mediums hit hard; A erodes while Yellow is busy attacking n3.
5. Once A falls, swing onto Y.
Win at T+~85s.

## AI behavior
A's first replan picks n2 (nearest neutral). Y's first replan picks n3. Both ignore each other initially (mountain LOS-blocks them apart). They turn toward the player only after the neutrals settle.

## Risks
If player attacks Yellow first, A gets free runway. Pacing matters. The central mountain is a "Berlin Wall" — necessary so the two AIs don't trivially trade and let the player free-farm.
