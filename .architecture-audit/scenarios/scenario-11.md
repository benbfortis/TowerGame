# Tempo Trap (Scenario 11)
**Focus:** tempo trap
**Difficulty:** medium
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize grabbing the close medium n1 costs more pop than expected and the AI rushes through n3 while they are committed.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: DELAY n1 grab. Drag P -> n2 (left light) and P -> n3 (right light) simultaneously to deny AI's right-flank lane.
2. Lights arrive at n2 (~3s) and n3 (~5s). Capture both.
3. Now drag n3 -> n1 (the bait medium) — capture from a stronger angle.
4. n1 captured. Drag n1 (medium) -> A. Mediums grind A down.
Win at T+~90s.

## AI behavior
A's first replan picks n3 (nearest unowned, weakest). If player took n3 first, AI swaps to n1 (the bait) — and now player has tempo to attack instead of recover.

## Risks
Anti-solution: grabbing n1 first feels obvious. Player who does it loses 3 pop seeding n1 and AI captures n3 then pours into P. The brick wall on the left forces the right-flank race; without it, players sidestep the dilemma.
