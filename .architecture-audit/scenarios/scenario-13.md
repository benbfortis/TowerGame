# Forced Hold (Scenario 13)
**Focus:** forced defense
**Difficulty:** medium
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize routing the back neutral n0 INTO P (instead of attacking) is the only way to survive the opening wave.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag n0 -> P. Wait — n0 is neutral, can't drag from it. Correction: drag P -> n0 to capture the back seed first.
2. P emits lights toward n0 (~120px / 120 = 1s travel). n0 was pop 0, captures at T+~2s.
3. Drag n0 -> P (NOW player owns n0). Inflows feed P pop. P regenerates.
4. With P stable, drag P -> n1 (left flank light).
5. n1 captures. Chain n1 -> A1 (the closer AI medium).
6. A1 captures (mediums hit at 3 dmg; with n1 + P feeding, A1 falls).
Win at T+~110s.

## AI behavior
A1 and A2 both replan toward P (closest target). With two mediums emitting, P would die in ~30s without seed. The brick wall on the right corridor funnels both AIs into the n1 lane, where the player can counter-press once n0 is feeding.

## Risks
Anti-solution: attacking A1 directly. P drains pop emitting lights toward A1, AI mediums arrive at P faster than lights at A1, P falls.
