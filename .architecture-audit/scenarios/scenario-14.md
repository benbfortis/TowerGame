# Sharpshot Alley (Scenario 14)
**Focus:** ranged bottleneck
**Difficulty:** medium
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize hugging the left flank breaks Yellow's sharpshooter LOS to the lane.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n1 (left-flank light). The mountain strip immediately east of n1 blocks Yellow LOS to this lane.
2. n1 captures ~T+3s. Drag n1 -> n2 (centre medium, ABOVE the mountain strip).
3. Units travel from n1 to n2 along the left edge — still safe from Y's snipers.
4. n2 captures ~T+12s. Drag n2 -> A.
5. Mediums from n2 reach A. Y's snipers can only fire across the mountain at units exposed in the open — by now too late.
Win at T+~80s.

## AI behavior
A replans toward n2 (the contested medium). Y (sharpshooter loadout: factoryDamage 0 but high unit damage) fires at any player units in its LOS. Player who avoids the kill zone neutralises Y entirely.

## Risks
Anti-solution: drag P -> n2 directly. Path crosses Yellow's LOS — lights die in transit. The mountain LOS-blocker is the entire puzzle.
