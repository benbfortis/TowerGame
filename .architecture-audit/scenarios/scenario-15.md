# Two Fronts (Scenario 15)
**Focus:** multi-front pressure (3-faction kingmaker)
**Difficulty:** medium-hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize patience lets A and Y exhaust each other on n3 before the player swings in.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n1 and P -> n2 (flank lights, low commitment).
2. Both capture ~T+3s. Hold position; do NOT push n3 yet.
3. A and Y both target n3 (medium, central, equidistant). They trade hits — both bleed pop.
4. T+~25s: A or Y captures n3 at low pop. Player drags n1 or n2 -> n3 to swoop in (n3 likely now at pop 1-3, easily flipped).
5. n3 owned by player. Drag n3 -> A (or Y — whichever is weaker). Mediums grind down.
6. Sweep the other.
Win at T+~120s.

## AI behavior
A and Y both first-replan toward n3. The brick wall above n3 LOS-blocks A from reaching Y directly through the centre — both pour into n3 instead. They make the player's move for them.

## Risks
If player attacks too early, they get caught between two angry AIs. The wall is sized so neither AI can sidestep it cheaply.
