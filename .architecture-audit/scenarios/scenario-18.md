# Friendly Anchor (Scenario 18)
**Focus:** friendly anchor
**Difficulty:** medium-hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize feeding pB (the back heavy) with lights from P turns the rear base into the win condition.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> pB. Lights from P flow into the back heavy, adding pop.
2. T+0s simultaneously drag pB -> n2 (right medium). pB has pop 12 at start — emits one heavy at pop 8 cost. Heavy arrives at n2 ~T+10s.
3. n2 captures. Chain n2 -> A.
4. Meanwhile lights continue P -> pB, pop accumulates, pB streams more heavies.
5. Heavies reach n2, then A. The medium mountain wedge blocks A from rushing pB directly.
Win at T+~100s.

## AI behavior
A's first replan picks P (closer than pB). With the mountain wedge in the way, A's units route around — buying time. AI applies pressure on P but cannot reach pB directly.

## Risks
Anti-solution: ignoring pB. With no feed, pB emits one heavy and stalls (pop drops to 4, can't afford another heavy emit at cost 8). The whole back base sits idle.
