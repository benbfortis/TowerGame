# Roadblock (Scenario 19)
**Focus:** heavy roadblock
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize lights bouncing off A's heavy is a waste; only a captured n3 (heavy) can output enough damage to break A.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n1 (left medium). n1 captures at ~T+3s.
2. Drag P -> n2 (right light). Captures ~T+4s.
3. Chain n1 + n2 -> n3 (central heavy neutral). Mediums and lights pile on. n3 captures around T+15-20s.
4. Drag P -> n3 to keep feeding (n3 needs pop 8 to emit a heavy).
5. n3 emits heavies. Drag n3 -> aM (AI medium) to clear it (T+~40s).
6. With aM down, drag n3 -> A. Heavies smash A.
Win at T+~120s.

## AI behavior
A is heavy (9s cadence). aM is medium (5s). A's first replan picks n3 (the contested heavy). AI streams mediums and heavies — race to capture n3 first. Brick L-shape walls force player approach through the centre column.

## Risks
Anti-solution: lights all-in on A. A is heavy with high HP; light damage (1) chip-misses against A's pop refill. Wasted tempo.
