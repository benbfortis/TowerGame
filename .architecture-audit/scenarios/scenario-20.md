# Cross Hairs (Scenario 20)
**Focus:** yellow spoiler (3-faction kingmaker)
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize being the second-strongest faction makes Yellow attack the AI instead.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n2 (central medium). Capture by T+~5s.
2. DO NOT push hard early. Let A and Y posture.
3. Yellow's brain picks the weakest non-same target — that's whichever of P/A has fewer factories. If you stay at 2 factories while A grabs n1 (3 factories), Y attacks A.
4. Once Y starts attacking A, drag n2 + P -> A. Two-pronged on A.
5. With A weakened, Y may swing toward P — but A falls first if timing is tight.
Win at T+~130s.

## AI behavior
A first-replans toward n2. Y first-replans also toward n2 (equidistant). The mountain wall between A and Y means neither auto-targets the other; both pick P or n2. Player's positional balance decides the kingmaker arrow.

## Risks
If player overcommits early, both AIs gang up on them. Yellow's swing relies on factory-count parity — too many captures and you become the target.
