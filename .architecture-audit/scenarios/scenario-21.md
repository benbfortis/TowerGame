# Race the Clock (Scenario 21)
**Focus:** race-the-clock
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize the aH heavy will start dumping on them at T+30s and the only counter is being IN A's base by then.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n2 (right medium — closest high-value).
2. Simultaneously drag P -> n1 (left light, anchor).
3. n2 captures ~T+5s. Drag n2 -> n1 to consolidate.
4. From n2, drag n2 -> aH (the AI's heavy chain). Mediums hit aH for 3 dmg each.
5. Mountain band forces approach via flanks. Take the right side (closer from n2).
6. aH captures around T+~50s. Drag aH (now player heavy) -> A. Heavies finish A.
Win at T+~75s.

## AI behavior
aH starts emitting heavies at T+9s. A (medium) emits mediums at T+5s. AI replans pick n2 (closer) then P. Mountain band blocks direct rushes; AI takes flanks. Tight timing.

## Risks
If player wastes the first 10s on positioning, aH's heavies arrive at P around T+20s and the run is lost. Speed-of-decision matters.
