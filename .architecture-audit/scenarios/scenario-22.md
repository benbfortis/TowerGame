# Four Corners (Scenario 22)
**Focus:** multi-front pressure (4-faction)
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize Green is the immediate threat (adjacent corner) and Yellow + AI will trade on the n3 gate while Green is dealt with.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n1 (lower-centre gate). Captures by T+4s.
2. Drag P -> nL (left lane medium, friendly direction). Captures by T+7s.
3. Drag n1 + P -> G (the adjacent enemy corner). G captures around T+30-40s.
4. Meanwhile Y and A trade on n3 and nR/nL. They don't touch P for the first ~25s (brick walls partition routes).
5. After G falls, swing toward the weaker of Y/A. n2 (centre heavy) becomes the staging ground.
Win at T+~150s.

## AI behavior
G first-replans toward n1 (its nearest neutral, also nearest to P). A and Y first-replan toward n2 (contested centre heavy). Brick partitions mean A targets n3/nR, Y targets n3/nL. The cross of brick is sized so each faction has natural neighbours.

## Risks
4 factions on 9 factories is the operator's stated maximum. Risk of chaotic feel if Green is captured too slowly — player gets pincered. The "kill Green first" timing is tight.
