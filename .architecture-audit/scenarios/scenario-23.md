# Heavy Highway (Scenario 23)
**Focus:** resource island + chain
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize holding BOTH island heavies (i1 + i2) creates a heavy stream the AI cannot match.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag pH -> i1 (one of the two island heavies). pH is heavy, crosses chasm at 0.5×.
2. Simultaneously drag pH -> i2. Split heavies between both islands.
3. i1 captures ~T+10s (one heavy lands, pop 0 -> 1). i2 captures ~T+12s.
4. Chain P (medium) -> pH and pn -> pH to feed heavy pop.
5. Both islands streaming heavies. Drag i1 -> aH (AI heavy) and i2 -> an. Two-pronged across the chasm.
6. aH falls ~T+50s. an captured. Then drag survivors -> A.
Win at T+~150s.

## AI behavior
AI heavies (aH) target i1/i2 (closest reachable). AI streams across the chasm too. Symmetric island race; player needs to commit both pH outputs.

## Risks
Anti-solution: focus only on i1. AI captures i2, becomes a third heavy producer, eclipses player. Both islands must be contested simultaneously.
