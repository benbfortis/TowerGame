# Chess Match (Scenario 24)
**Focus:** backdoor + risk/reward (3-faction)
**Difficulty:** hard
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize the backdoor through n2 sneaks behind A while Yellow is busy on the short front.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n4 (short-front gate, draws Y's attention).
2. Simultaneously drag P -> n1 (backdoor entry on the left).
3. n4 captures ~T+5s. Y replans, targets n4 (closest contested).
4. n1 captures ~T+5s. Chain n1 -> n2 (deep backdoor, behind mountain LOS-block).
5. n2 captures ~T+15s. Y has no LOS to n2 (mountain blocks). A is focused on n4 contest.
6. Drag n2 -> n3 (close to A). n3 captures ~T+25s.
7. Drag n3 -> A. Mediums arrive from behind. A falls ~T+60s.
8. Sweep Y.
Win at T+~130s.

## AI behavior
A first-replans toward n4 (closest contested). Y first-replans toward n4 too (or n2 if backdoor units are sighted). The mountain hides the backdoor lane from Y's LOS — Y commits to the wrong front.

## Risks
If player doesn't open n4 as a feint, Y wanders into the backdoor lane. The feint is the load-bearing trick.
