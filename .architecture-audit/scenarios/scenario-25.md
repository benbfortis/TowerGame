# Last Stand (Scenario 25)
**Focus:** multi-front pressure (4-faction siege, hardest)
**Difficulty:** hardest
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize pressuring n3 forces all three AIs to converge there, letting them kill each other while you flank.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag pH -> n3 (central heavy). pH is heavy (pop 12). First heavy at cost 8 emits immediately.
2. Drag P -> n1 and P -> n2 (flank lights, capture by T+5s).
3. n3 captures ~T+15-20s as pH heavies overwhelm.
4. The brick partitions above mean Y, A, G must come through their respective corridors to reach n3. They contest each other AND n3 simultaneously.
5. Mountain bands across the middle prevent player rushing top-down — player feeds n3 and waits.
6. AIs trade on n3 and the upper corridors. Y vs A (closest neighbours), G vs A (other neighbours). Player swoops on the survivor.
7. Once one AI falls (likely Y or G, the mediums), drag n3 -> remaining AI bases.
Win at T+~200s.

## AI behavior
Y first-replans toward n4 (its closest neutral). G first-replans toward n5. A (heavy) first-replans toward n3. The brick partitions in the upper map sized so each AI has a single primary corridor; the mountain bands across the middle keep them apart from the player. Player wins by being the last alive, not the strongest.

## Risks
Hardest scenario. 4 factions on 10 factories. AI replan loops are vulnerable to "everyone targets centre" deadlock — if pacing feels off in playtest, widen the upper partitions or remove one brick wall to let A and G fight more readily.
