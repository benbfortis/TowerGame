# Outnumbered (Scenario 7)
**Focus:** asymmetric start
**Difficulty:** easy-medium
**Clever moment:** The player feels clever when they realize capturing the medium n1 first matches the AI's medium throughput before A2 can pile on.

## Intended solution
1. T+0s: drag P -> n1 (the medium neutral on the left flank, the higher-value prize).
2. P's lights route around the central mountain (light is blocked by mountain). First arrives ~3-4s.
3. n1 captured at ~T+5s with multiple lights. Now player has a medium (5s cadence, +3 dmg/hit).
4. Drag n1 -> A1 (the AI medium, weaker after a few emits). Mediums hit for 3, capture A1 in ~4 hits.
5. Mop up A2 with combined fire from P and n2 (capture n2 mid-game).
Win at T+~70s.

## AI behavior
First replan: A1 targets n1 (nearest neutral). A2 targets n2 or P. AI applies real pressure from two fronts — player who only defends loses tempo.

## Risks
If player misroutes and tries straight P->A through mountain, lights stall at the mountain edge. The mountain LOS-blocks the centre so player can't even target A directly from P early on.
